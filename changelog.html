Changelog

Every update of Fixa, with the same notes you see when it arrives. Fixa installs them by itself – click a version to get a link to it.

Fixa 0.11 Latest

November 1, 2026 · build 11 · macOS 26 or later

  • While Settings, a dialog or any other window is open on top, the note behind it can no longer be scrolled or changed.
  • Clicking outside the Search panel (⌘F) now closes it.

Fixa 0.10

November 1, 2026 · build 10 · macOS 26 or later

  • After an update, the Dock and Finder show Fixa’s new icon right away – no leftover old icon.

Fixa 0.9

November 1, 2026 · build 9 · macOS 26 or later

  • A new app icon: a page of notes with a yellow top and a stroke of the highlighter.
  • The document preview now scrolls all the way – PDFs page after page, Word and text documents, and web pages.
  • HTML files show a preview of the page in your note, just like PDFs.

Fixa 0.8

November 1, 2026 · build 8 · macOS 26 or later

  • Add documents to your notes – PDFs, Word, Pages and more, up to 100 MB. Drag them in, paste them, or click File (⇧⌘A).
  • A document shows a preview of its first page or a small card, with its name and size. Drag the corner to resize it.
  • Click a document to read it right in Fixa. The arrow on it sets what a click opens and what Finder shows – for all documents in Settings › Documents.

Fixa 0.7

November 1, 2026 · build 7 · macOS 26 or later

  • More room above your notes: the text starts a little lower under the formatting bar.
  • When a new version of Fixa is ready, an arrow appears next to Settings at the bottom left – click it to update, even after choosing Later.

Fixa 0.6

October 31, 2026 · build 6 · macOS 26 or later

  • Fixa checks for updates once a day, and only while you’re using it – never in the background.

Fixa 0.5

October 31, 2026 · build 5 · macOS 26 or later

  • Fixa can’t be quit or closed while an update downloads and installs, so the update always finishes cleanly – you can still cancel the download.
  • Logging out or shutting down during an update safely stops it, and Fixa offers it again next time.

Fixa 0.4

October 30, 2026 · build 4 · macOS 26 or later

  • New installs start with a full Fixa Guide – short chapters with screenshots on projects, notes and pinning, formatting, links, images, search, shortcuts, sync and settings.
  • The example projects now include images, so you can try resizing and moving them right away.

Fixa 0.3

October 29, 2026 · build 3 · macOS 26 or later

  • Set Up Fixa is clearer: one-time steps like moving to Applications or turning on iCloud have their own button.
  • Opening at login and automatic updates switch on and off right away, with an On / Off label.
  • Set Up Everything does all the remaining steps at once.

Fixa 0.2

October 29, 2026 · build 2 · macOS 26 or later

  • Exact sync between your Macs: every change is versioned and logged, deletions spread safely, and Fixa asks when it can’t be sure.
  • History (⌘Y) shows every change on all your Macs and brings deleted notes back. Sync Decisions (⌥⌘Y) for notes deleted on one Mac but changed on another.
  • Turning on iCloud sync shows exactly what merging will do before anything changes.
  • Fixa now updates itself – you see what’s new and install it with one click.
  • A new welcome screen sets everything up in one click: Applications, opening at login, iCloud and updates.
  • New installs start with a Fixa Guide project and a few example projects.
